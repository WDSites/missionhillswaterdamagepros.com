Why water heaters leak

Tank heaters corrode from the inside, and a rusted tank will eventually weep at the seams or at the base. Sediment builds up on the bottom, and a recent LADWP drinking water quality report lists total hardness of about 103 mg/L for the San Fernando Valley and western Los Angeles area, which is moderately hard water that leaves scale. Valves, connectors and the temperature and pressure relief line can also fail.

California Health and Safety Code section 19211 requires residential water heaters to be braced, anchored or strapped against earthquake motion. Strapping does not stop a leak, but a heater that tips can tear its own pipes loose. The same statute asks home sellers to certify compliance.

Where the water ends up

Garage or utility closet

Water spreads across concrete and finds the lowest point, often a drain or the door threshold.

Attic or upper closet

A leaking unit above living space soaks insulation and the ceiling below.

Wall behind the heater

Water can wick into drywall and the bottom plate and stay hidden.

Neighboring rooms

Water slips under walls into carpet and cabinets next door.

The California Plumbing Code addresses drainage pans for water heaters installed where a leak would damage the building, such as an attic or a floor-ceiling assembly. A pan with a drain line reduces damage but does not replace maintenance. Ask a licensed plumber what your installation needs.

First hour safety steps for a leaking water heater

  1. Turn off the cold water supply valve on top of the heater. If it will not turn, close the main valve for the house.
  2. For a gas heater, turn the gas control to off or the pilot setting. If you smell gas, leave the house and call 911.
  3. For an electric heater, switch off its breaker only if you can reach the panel from a dry spot.
  4. Do not touch the heater or any outlet while standing in water.
  5. Photograph the leak and the heater label, then call a plumber and the 24/7 line.

Hot water and gas are both hazards

The water can scald, and a gas smell means leave first. Never relight a heater that has been wet.

How we clean up after a water heater leak

1

Clear the pan and floor

Water is pumped or vacuumed off the garage slab or closet floor, and the drain pan area is cleared out.

2

Check the shared wall

A meter on the wall behind the heater and the cabinets nearby shows whether water crept past the puddle.

3

Open the bottom of the wall

Baseboard and a strip of wet drywall come off so air movers can reach the bottom plate.

4

Watch the readings

Dehumidifiers run and the numbers are written down at each visit until the materials are dry.

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the reference standard for professional water damage work, and we mention it only as background reading. Rebuilding drywall or flooring afterward belongs on the water damage repair page.

What changes the cost of a water heater cleanup

Location

A garage floor dries quickly, while a heater above a ceiling means opening finished surfaces.

Duration

A slow leak that went unnoticed wets more material than a sudden failure caught at once.

Flooring

Concrete, tile and carpet each need different handling.

Adjacent rooms

Water that crossed a wall adds equipment and time.

Insurance fit and permits

A sudden tank failure is often covered as accidental discharge, while a slow leak from a neglected unit often is not. The line between the two matters, so photograph the heater and note when you found the water. The Los Angeles Department of Building and Safety says a plumbing permit is generally required to replace a water heater at a single-family home unless a registered contractor does a like-for-like swap, so ask your plumber how the permit is handled.

Mistakes to avoid and prevention

  • Do not turn the heater back on until a plumber has checked it.
  • Do not ignore rust streaks, a damp base or a hissing sound.
  • Flush sediment and check the relief valve on a schedule your plumber recommends.
  • Confirm the heater is strapped and that the pan, if installed, drains.

If the failure was a supply line instead of the tank, see burst pipe water damage.

Water heater leak questions

Is emergency water heater leak cleanup available in Mission Hills, CA?

Yes. Someone answers day and night, so a heater that fails at 2 a.m. can be reported right away. Shut off the water and the fuel first, and a crew is sent that day when one is free.

Do you replace the water heater?

No, a licensed plumber handles the heater. We pump out the water and dry the garage or closet around it.

Is it safe to touch the heater after it leaks?

Not while standing in water or near powered outlets. Turn off the water supply from a dry spot, and keep clear of gas smells.

Will the drywall behind the heater be okay?

Often, if it is dried promptly. Drywall that stayed wet longer, or that crumbles, usually has to be cut out and replaced.
