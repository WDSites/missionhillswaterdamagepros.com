What storms do in the San Fernando Valley

The National Weather Service reports a 1991 to 2020 annual precipitation normal of 14.25 inches for Downtown Los Angeles. Much of it arrives in a few storms, and dry ground sheds water fast.

Rainfall totals from the February 24 and 25, 2023 storm, as reported by local television, ran from 5.00 inches at Northridge to 10.56 inches at Woodland Hills. Those are nearby places, not Mission Hills readings. The point for a homeowner is simple: a single storm can deliver a year of runoff in a day, so drainage that looks fine in a dry spell may not cope.

The Valley floor here slopes gently, and a public reference puts Mission Hills near 913 feet in elevation. Water running off hillsides and streets can collect at low corners, driveways and garage doors before it finds a drain.

Where storm water gets in

Garage doors and thresholds

Water runs down a driveway and under a door, then spreads across the floor and into connected rooms.

Windows and sliding doors

Wind-driven rain pushes past worn seals and weep holes and soaks sills and walls.

Walls below overflowing gutters

Water that spills against a wall can seep into stucco, siding and the framing behind it.

Low yard areas

Ponding against a foundation can find its way through slab edges and vents.

First hour storm damage safety steps

  1. Stay out of rooms where water reaches outlets or appliances, and cut that circuit only from a dry floor.
  2. Do not walk or drive through flowing water outside. It is deeper and faster than it looks.
  3. Move belongings off wet floors and lift rugs. Keep children and pets away from the water.
  4. Photograph the entry points and the damage, including dates and times.
  5. Call the 24/7 line so extraction and drying start while the weather clears.

Storm water may be contaminated

Water that ran across streets or yards can carry dirt and waste. Avoid contact, wash hands well and tell the dispatcher where the water came from.

How we handle storm water losses

1

Pumps and extraction units clear water from the garage slab, hallway and carpeted rooms.

The meter follows water back from the wet floor to the door, window or threshold where it entered.

Numbers are recorded each visit until materials match a dry reference.

ANSI/IICRC S500, the standard we point to as a reference and not as a credential, covers how storm-wetted structures are dried. Wetter storm seasons can leave materials damp for days, so drying matters before mold takes hold. See mold remediation if growth has already started.

What changes the cost of storm damage cleanup

Water category

Runoff that crossed yards and streets is handled differently from clean rain.

Rooms affected

A garage alone is simpler than water that reached bedrooms and hallways.

Flooring

Concrete dries quickly, while carpet pad and wood often need removal.

Time since the storm

Water left in place for days spreads and encourages mold.

Insurance fit for storm and flood

Rain that enters through a storm-damaged opening may be covered under a standard homeowners policy, while surface water rising from the ground is usually treated as flood and needs a separate flood policy. The line between them can be thin. Photograph how the water entered and keep dated records of the storm.

Mistakes to avoid and prevention

  • Do not wait for the rain to stop before calling.
  • Clear gutters and downspouts before the rainy season.
  • Check door and window seals and garage thresholds each fall.
  • Keep sandbags or barriers ready if your driveway slopes toward the garage.
