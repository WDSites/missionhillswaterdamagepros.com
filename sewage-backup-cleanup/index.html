Sewage backup is the water loss nobody wants to describe. Wastewater rises through a floor drain, a tub or a toilet, and it carries bacteria and other contaminants that make it a health matter as well as a property matter. In Mission Hills, CA call (818) 337-2642 if sewage is coming up, and keep people and pets out of the area.

We handle the cleanup, removal of contaminated materials and drying that follow a backup. Clearing the blockage and repairing the sewer line are plumbing jobs. The two usually happen together, with the plumber restoring flow first.

Who owns what in a Los Angeles sewer

LA Sanitation says the city owns the public main, while the property owner maintains the private lateral from the building out to the middle of the street. That split matters, because a backup caused by a blockage in the lateral is the owner's to fix, and one caused by a main blockage may be a city matter. LA Sanitation runs a 24/7 Customer Care Center at 1-800-773-2489 for sewer odors and reports.

The department advises hiring a licensed plumber for lateral problems and for backwater valves. A plumber can run a camera to see where the line is blocked and decide whether the problem is the lateral or the main.

Signs of a sewage backup

Gurgling drains

Bubbling in a tub or toilet when another fixture runs can mean a blocked line.

Water in the lowest drain

Wastewater appearing in a floor drain or shower is a common first sign.

Sewer odor

A persistent smell indoors with no visible water may mean gas is escaping a dry trap or damaged pipe.

Multiple fixtures slow

When several drains slow at once, the blockage is in the main line, not one fixture.

First hour sewage safety steps

  1. Stop using water in the house, including toilets, sinks, showers and washing machines.
  2. Keep children, pets and anyone with a weakened immune system out of the affected area.
  3. Do not touch the water with bare skin, and keep power off in wet areas where the panel is dry and easy to reach.
  4. Do not run a household vacuum or fans on wet sewage, which can spread contaminants.
  5. Call a plumber for the blockage and the 24/7 line for cleanup, and report odors to LA Sanitation if the main may be involved.

Treat sewage water as contaminated

Even a small backup can carry bacteria. Wash hands thoroughly after any contact, and ask a physician about symptoms instead of guessing.

How we clean up after a sewage backup

1

Isolate the area

Barriers keep contaminated water from reaching clean rooms.

2

Remove water and soft materials

Wastewater is pumped out, and carpet, pad and wet drywall that soaked it up are taken out for disposal.

3

Clean and disinfect

Hard surfaces are cleaned, then treated with a product chosen for that surface.

4

Dry and recheck

Drying equipment runs, and readings are taken until the structure reaches a dry reference.

The trade standard ANSI/IICRC S500, listed here as a reference only, puts sewage in its most contaminated water category. Porous materials that sat in it are normally discarded, not cleaned. For the mold that can follow, see mold remediation.

What changes the cost of sewage cleanup

Volume and spread

A bathroom floor differs greatly from a backup that reached several rooms.

Materials affected

Carpet, pad and drywall that must be removed add disposal and rebuilding.

Duration

The longer contaminated water sits, the more material it wets and the stronger the odor.

Containment needs

Protecting clean areas adds time and supplies.

Insurance fit for sewage backup

Standard homeowners policies often exclude sewer or drain backup unless an endorsement was added. If you have one, it may carry a lower limit than the rest of the policy. Read your coverage, document the damage with photos and keep records of what was discarded.

Mistakes to avoid and prevention

  • Do not flush or run water while the line is blocked.
  • Do not pour grease down kitchen drains or flush wipes.
  • Ask a plumber about a camera inspection of an older lateral and a backwater valve.
  • Keep roots from growing toward the line, and know where your cleanout is.

Sewage backup questions

Is emergency sewage backup cleanup available in Mission Hills, CA?

Yes. Stop using water, keep people away and call at any hour. A contained cleanup crew goes out the same day when one is free.

Do you clear the blockage?

No. A licensed plumber clears the blockage and repairs the line. We clean up the contaminated area and dry the structure.

Can carpet be saved after a sewage backup?

Usually not. Carpet and pad that have been soaked by sewage are porous and are commonly removed and discarded.

Who is responsible for the sewer line?

LA Sanitation owns the public main, and the owner maintains the private lateral. A plumber can tell you where the blockage is.
