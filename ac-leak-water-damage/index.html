An air conditioner leak usually starts small. A clogged condensate line backs up, a drain pan overflows or a coil freezes and melts, and the water lands in a ceiling, a closet or a wall. In Mission Hills, CA the cooling season is long, so these leaks are a regular source of ceiling stains. Call (818) 337-2642 when a drip or a sagging ceiling appears below the unit.

We extract the water and dry what it reached. Repairing the cooling system is a job for an HVAC technician, and we do not do it. This page covers ceiling units, closet and attic air handlers and wall-mounted units.

How an air conditioner leaks inside a house

Cooling removes moisture from the air, and that water leaves through a condensate drain. When the line clogs with algae and dust, the water has nowhere to go and spills from the pan. A dirty filter can freeze the coil, and the melting ice adds to the flow. Disconnected or cracked drain lines do the same.

The National Weather Service reports a July average high of 82.0 degrees Fahrenheit for Downtown Los Angeles in its 1991 to 2020 normals, and inland Valley neighborhoods keep cooling equipment running through much of the summer.

Many of the 5,726 housing units the Census counts in ZIP 91345 have central air in a closet, a garage or an attic. How common leaks are here is hard to put in a number, so treat any claim of exact frequency with caution.

Signs that an air conditioner is leaking

Ceiling stain under the air handler

A brown ring below an attic or closet unit often follows a clogged drain pan.

Water around the indoor unit

Puddles, dampness or a dripping vent mean condensate is not leaving.

Unit stops cooling

A frozen coil can block airflow, and a float switch may shut the system off.

Musty smell from vents

Standing moisture in the drain pan or duct can produce an odor.

First hour AC leak safety steps

  1. Turn the system off at the thermostat so it stops making more condensate.
  2. With a sagging ceiling or a wet light fixture, leave the room alone and let an electrician or the utility advise before any switch is touched.
  3. Place a bucket under any drip and move belongings out of the way.
  4. Do not remove ceiling material yourself if it is bulging, because it can be heavy with water.
  5. Photograph the damage, then call an HVAC technician and the 24/7 line.

How we handle the water damage from an AC leak

1

Handle the water first

Standing water is removed, and a swollen ceiling section is released carefully so it does not come down.

2

Measure around the unit

Meters and the thermal camera show how far wet insulation and drywall extend from the air handler.

3

Take out soaked insulation

Insulation and soft drywall that cannot dry are removed to expose the framing.

4

Dry the ceiling space

Air movers and a dehumidifier run until the ceiling cavity reads like the rest of the house.

The published reference for professional water damage work is ANSI/IICRC S500, and it is named here for background only. LADBS requires a mechanical permit for HVAC installation or modification, so replacing a unit that leaked brings the permit question with it. Ceiling patching afterward is on the water damage repair page.

What changes the cost of AC leak repairs

Where the unit is

An attic or ceiling unit means more insulation and drywall to handle than a floor closet.

How long it dripped

A long slow drip soaks more material than a single overflow.

Ceiling condition

Sagging or stained drywall often has to be replaced.

Mold risk

A ceiling that stayed damp may need a mold check before closing up.

Insurance fit

Policies sometimes cover a one-time overflow from a cooling system, but gradual leaks and skipped maintenance are often excluded. Policies differ on equipment and on water damage, so read yours before deciding what to claim. Note the dates, keep photos and keep the service invoice from your HVAC technician.

Mistakes to avoid and prevention

  • Do not keep running a system that is dripping.
  • Change filters on schedule and ask an HVAC technician to flush the condensate line.
  • Ask whether a secondary drain pan or float switch is installed on attic units.
  • Check ceilings below attic equipment at the start of each cooling season.

AC leak questions

Is emergency AC leak water damage service available in Mission Hills, CA?

Yes. Switch the system off at the thermostat and call at any hour. If a crew is free it goes out the same day.

Do you repair the air conditioner?

No. An HVAC technician fixes the equipment. We dry the ceiling, insulation and walls that the overflow reached.

Why is there a stain under my attic unit?

The condensate pan or drain line is the usual cause. Water spills, soaks insulation and shows up as a ring on the ceiling below.

Is the stain a mold problem?

Not necessarily, but a ceiling that stays damp can grow mold. A moisture reading shows whether it is still wet.
