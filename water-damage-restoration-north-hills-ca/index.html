North Hills is a neighborhood in the City of Los Angeles, in the north San Fernando Valley, and Mission Hills is its northern neighbor. If you have water damage in North Hills, call (818) 337-2642 and describe what is wet. This page explains how water losses are handled here, and which nearby pages cover related situations.

Where North Hills sits

The City of Los Angeles Planning Department groups North Hills with Mission Hills and Panorama City in one community plan area. Council district materials describe the neighborhood as formerly called Mission Acres and later Sepulveda, and the Sepulveda name still appears in postal records for ZIP 91343. That is why some callers give an address with one name and a ZIP with another.

District maps place it roughly between Balboa Boulevard and Bull Creek on the west, Lassen Street on the north and Roscoe Boulevard on the south. Sepulveda Boulevard is the long commercial street through the area. Boundaries are approximate, so give the dispatcher your street address instead of a neighborhood name.

Housing and what it means for water damage

For ZIP 91343, published census-based estimates show that about 35 percent of homes were built between 1940 and 1959, and about 34 percent between 1960 and 1979. Those figures describe the ZIP, not each block, and they are estimates. They do point to housing that is several decades old.

In older homes, supply lines, shutoff valves, water heater connectors and drain lines are more likely to be past their service life. A pinhole in an old copper line or a cracked washing machine hose can soak a wall for days before a stain appears. Check under sinks and behind appliances if a room smells musty.

Older plumbing

Mid-century supply lines and valves can fail slowly. Look for green crust, drips and damp cabinet floors.

Slab construction

Many Valley homes sit on concrete slabs, where a hidden leak shows up as warm floor spots or a running meter.

Water from outside

Rain that enters a garage or back door is treated differently from a pipe leak, so say which one you have.

Mold follows moisture

Materials that stay wet for several days can grow mold, which is a separate job from drying.

Drainage and the Pacoima Wash

Public sources note that North Hills was prone to flooding from the Pacoima Wash before the area was developed. Today the wash is a concrete flood channel that crosses the north Valley. That history is background, not a flood claim about any address.

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. Flood zones are mapped by exact address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center, so look yours up there rather than assuming from the neighborhood name.

First steps when water is spreading

Stop the source

Close the main shutoff by turning it clockwise. If it will not turn, call and say so.

Mind the electricity

Switch off breakers for wet rooms only when the panel is dry and easy to reach. If not, stay clear and call 911 or the power utility.

Photograph first

Take photos and short video of the damage, the source and damaged belongings before anything is moved.

Avoid dirty water

Keep children and pets away from water that came from a drain or from outside.

Extraction, drying and what to document

Restoration begins by finding the source and removing standing water. Wet carpet pad, baseboards and lower drywall are then checked with a moisture meter, and air movers and dehumidifiers dry what can be saved. Readings are taken again over the following days. Work of this kind is commonly guided by the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard.

For insurance, keep your photos, the date and time you found the damage, receipts for emergency repairs and a list of damaged items. Ask your insurer what proof it needs. Documentation helps your claim, but no restoration company controls how an insurer decides.

Services that match common North Hills problems

A burst or leaking supply line is covered on the burst pipe water damage page, and a failed tank on the water heater leak cleanup page. Standing water is handled under emergency water removal. Drying in the days after is on the structural drying and dehumidification page.

If a drain backs up, see sewage backup cleanup. Walls that stay damp after drying can grow mold, and rebuilding drywall and floors comes after drying is finished.

North Hills water damage questions

Is North Hills the same as Sepulveda?

Council district materials say North Hills was once called Sepulveda, and ZIP 91343 still carries that postal name. Give your street address when you call, and the dispatcher will sort out the location.

Do you serve North Hills from Mission Hills?

Yes. The business address is in Mission Hills and North Hills is a neighboring Los Angeles neighborhood. Call with your address and the dispatcher will confirm what is possible.

Who supplies water and issues permits here?

As part of the City of Los Angeles, North Hills gets its water from LADWP. Larger rebuilds need permits from the city building department, known as LADBS.

Is my North Hills home in a flood zone?

This page cannot say. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. Check the address on the FEMA map service and ask your insurer about flood coverage.

What should I save for my insurance claim?

Photos, video, dates, repair receipts and an item list. Ask what else your insurer needs before you throw anything away.
