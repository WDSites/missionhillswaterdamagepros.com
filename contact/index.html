What to have ready when you call

  • Your address and nearest cross streets.
  • Where the water came from, if you know, and whether it is still running.
  • Which rooms are wet, and whether water reached outlets or the panel.
  • Whether the water looks clean, dirty or smells like sewage.
  • Whether you rent, own or manage the property.
  • Your insurance company name, if you plan to file a claim.

If you do not know some of these, call anyway. The dispatcher can ask questions and help you work it out. In an emergency, speed matters more than perfect information.

Electrical or gas risk

If water has reached outlets, a panel or a gas appliance, stay out of the area and call 911 or the utility first. Then call us. Do not enter standing water near electrical equipment.
