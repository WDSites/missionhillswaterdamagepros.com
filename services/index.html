Choosing a service is mostly about what is happening right now. Standing water is a different problem from a wet wall, and a rebuild is a different problem from mold. The table below helps you pick the page that matches your situation.

The six services at a glance

Emergency Water Removal

Pumps and extraction units take standing water out of floors and low rooms right away, including from burst pipes. The page also covers crawl space and whole-house flooding.

Water Damage Repair

Drywall, insulation, ceilings, wood floors and cabinets are rebuilt after the structure is dry. It explains ceiling and wall repair and hardwood floor cupping.

Flood Damage Restoration

Storm runoff, groundwater and sewage backups need a safety plan for contaminated water. Basement flooding and sewage cleanup are covered on this page.

Water Mitigation

The first 24 to 72 hours decide how much is lost. Moisture mapping, air movers and dehumidifiers dry the structure and stop the spread.

Water Damage Cleanup

Soaked materials, debris and contaminated surfaces are removed and sanitized, with odor control afterward.

Mold Remediation

Inspection, containment, removal and clearance checks for mold that followed moisture, in line with ANSI/IICRC S520.

Most real losses involve more than one stage. A burst pipe starts with removal, moves to mitigation and cleanup, and ends with repair. A dispatcher can help you decide where you are in that sequence.

How the services work together

Drying and rebuilding are linked. Closing a wall over damp framing invites mold, which is why mitigation comes before repair, and why mold shows up as the secondary service on this site. If a leak was slow, mold may already be part of the picture, and an inspection on the mold page explains the next steps.

No page promises an outcome with your insurer. Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered by homeowners insurance, while slow leaks and neglect often are not, and flood damage usually needs separate flood coverage. Check your policy, and ask the crew to document readings and photos for your claim.

What these services do not include

A restoration crew does not repair plumbing, fix a leaking roof or waterproof a foundation. Those are separate trades, and the crew will say so if your problem needs one. It also does not rent drying equipment for you to run on your own.

Safety checks before you touch anything

  • If water is near outlets, appliances or a light fixture, stay out and shut off power at the panel only from a dry spot.
  • Do not walk under a sagging or bulging ceiling.
  • Leave the area if you smell sewage, gas or burning, and call 911 for gas or fire.
  • Keep children and pets out of the wet room.
