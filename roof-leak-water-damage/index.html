A roof leak does its damage out of sight first. Water enters through a worn shingle, a cracked tile or a failed flashing, then runs along the decking into insulation and ceiling drywall before anyone sees a stain. In Mission Hills, CA the practical goal is to catch the wet path early and dry it. Call (818) 337-2642 if water is dripping or a ceiling is bulging.

This page covers the cleanup and drying that follow a roof leak. Repairing the roof itself is a job for a roofer, and we do not do it. Ceiling patching and painting afterward are covered on the water damage repair page.

Rain in the San Fernando Valley and what it does to roofs

Most of the annual rain falls in a handful of storms, so a roof that looks fine all year can fail in a single downpour. Local television reported 6.70 inches at Van Nuys during the storm of February 24 and 25, 2023.

The American Community Survey counts 5,726 housing units in ZIP 91345, and many carry roofs that have gone through decades of sun and short, heavy rain. Sun hardens roofing materials, and then a storm finds the weak seam. The Los Angeles Department of Building and Safety publishes information bulletins on roofing requirements, so ask the department about permits before a roof is replaced.

Clues that the water came from the roof

Stains after rain

A ceiling stain that appears or grows after a storm points to the roof instead of a pipe.

Stain near an outside wall or chimney

Flashing around penetrations is a common entry point for water.

Damp insulation in the attic

Wet or matted insulation shows where water has been collecting above the ceiling.

Daylight, lifted or missing roofing

Gaps, slipped tiles and curled shingles can be seen from the ground with binoculars.

Plumbing leaks can look similar, and the timing is the best clue. A stain that grows on dry days points toward pipes. Our water mitigation page explains how moisture mapping separates the two.

First hour roof leak safety steps

  1. Move furniture and electronics out from under the leak and place a bucket under any drip.
  2. If a light fixture is wet or the ceiling is sagging, keep out of the room. Cut that circuit at the panel only when the floor around the panel is dry.
  3. Do not walk on a wet roof or climb a ladder in rain. Call a roofer for a tarp.
  4. Photograph the stain, the ceiling and the attic if you can reach it safely.
  5. Call the 24/7 line so drying can start while you arrange the roof repair.

A sagging ceiling can fail suddenly

Water pools in the drywall and adds weight. Keep people and pets out from under a bulge until a technician has looked at it.

How we clean up and dry after a roof leak

1

Catch and drain

Buckets and extraction handle floor water, and a pooled ceiling bubble is drained through a small controlled opening.

2

Follow the stain upward

The thermal camera traces the damp path from the ceiling stain up to the attic edge and across the insulation.

3

Pull what stays wet

Matted insulation and crumbling drywall are removed so the joists and rafters can dry.

4

Dry the cavity

Air movement and dehumidification continue until the ceiling and attic readings match a dry room.

For readers who want the trade wording, ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published standard on structural drying, named here as a reference and nothing more. A roofer should confirm the roof is sealed before drying finishes, or the same path will wet again.

What changes the cost of roof leak cleanup

Size of the wet area

A stain over one room needs less equipment than a path that crossed several.

Attic insulation

Wet insulation is heavy and often has to be removed and replaced.

Ceiling condition

Water-damaged drywall can be dried if firm, but sagging or cracked sections usually come out.

Access

Low attics and tight cavities add labor.

Insurance fit for a roof leak

Homeowners policies commonly cover water damage from a sudden storm event, such as wind lifting roofing, but may not cover wear and tear or long neglected roofing. The roof repair and the interior damage may be treated differently. Keep dated photos, the roofer invoice and the storm date, and let your insurer weigh in before materials are thrown out.

Mistakes to avoid and prevention

  • Do not patch the stain with paint before the ceiling is dry.
  • Do not climb onto a wet or tile roof yourself.
  • Have a roofer check flashing and sealants before the rainy season, not during it.
  • Keep gutters clear so water does not back up under roofing edges.

If the stain follows rain, it may also be wind-driven water instead of a roof defect, which is covered on the storm water damage page.

Roof leak water damage questions

Is emergency roof leak water damage service available in Mission Hills, CA?

Yes. Calls are taken around the clock, storms included, and a dispatcher can explain how to contain the drip while a crew is arranged for the same day if one is free.

Do you repair the roof?

No, roofing belongs to a roofer. Our part starts inside, with extraction, drying and cleanup of what the leak soaked.

How do I know whether it was the roof or a pipe?

Timing is the first clue. A stain that grows after rain points to the roof, while one that grows on dry days points to plumbing. A moisture inspection can confirm it.

Can wet attic insulation be saved?

Usually not if it is saturated. Insulation holds water and stays wet for a long time, so it is commonly removed and replaced after the roof is fixed.
