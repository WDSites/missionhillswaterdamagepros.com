Wet carpet is one of the most common results of a leak, and one of the most fixable if it is handled quickly. The carpet fibers are only the top layer. Underneath sit the pad and the subfloor, and those hold the water. In Mission Hills, CA call (818) 337-2642 if carpet feels damp, squishes underfoot or smells sour.

This page is about carpet, pad and the floor under them. Sewage in carpet follows a stricter process, covered on the sewage backup cleanup page. Basements are uncommon in the San Fernando Valley, so most wet carpet here is on ground-level or upper floors.

What happens when carpet gets wet

Water soaks through the fibers into the pad, which acts like a sponge. Clean water can sometimes be extracted and the carpet dried in place, but the pad is cheap compared with the risk of mold, so it is often replaced. First hour carpet safety steps

  1. Stop the water source, and switch off the circuit for that room only from dry footing.
  2. Do not use a household vacuum on wet carpet. It is an electrical risk.
  3. Lift furniture off the carpet or put foil or plastic under the legs to stop staining.
  4. Keep children and pets off wet carpet.
  5. Photograph the damage and call the 24/7 line.

How we handle wet carpet

1

Extract fibers and pad

Portable or truck-mounted extraction pulls water from the carpet face and the pad beneath it.

2

Test below the surface

A meter checks the pad, the tack strip and the subfloor, because those hold water the longest.

3

Lift or remove

Carpet is lifted and held up on air to dry, or the pad is taken out when it cannot be saved.

4

Dry the floor

Dehumidifiers and air movers run, and readings are logged until the subfloor is dry.

The trade standard ANSI/IICRC S500 addresses carpet and pad decisions, and we mention it only as a reference. If wet carpet has lingered and mold is present, see mold remediation.

What changes the cost of wet carpet work

Water category

Clean, gray and contaminated water lead to different decisions.

Carpet age and backing

Older carpet with a glued backing is harder to lift and dry.

Area wet

A single room costs less than a hallway and several bedrooms.

Time wet

Carpet wet for days is harder to save than carpet extracted the same day.

Insurance fit

Sudden leaks that wet carpet are often covered. Slow leaks and floodwater from outside may not be. Keep the carpet or a sample if your insurer asks, photograph the area and note when the water was found.

Mistakes to avoid and prevention

  • Do not leave wet carpet in place for more than a day or two.
  • Do not rely on a fan alone to dry carpet and pad.
  • Do not walk on soaked carpet with shoes, which spreads dirt and stains.
  • Check carpet near water heaters, washers and exterior doors after storms.

Do you replace the carpet?

No. We extract and dry. If carpet or pad cannot be saved, a flooring installer replaces it.

Can I dry carpet myself with a wet vacuum?

A wet vacuum takes some surface water, but pad and subfloor stay wet. Only a meter reading shows dryness.

How long can carpet stay wet before mold grows?

Mold can begin within a day or two in warm, damp conditions, though it varies. Acting quickly lowers the risk.
